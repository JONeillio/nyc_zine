# Travel zines

Static zines, one per trip (New York at the root, Nicaragua in `/nicaragua`), plus a newsstand landing page at `index.html`. Each trip's content lives in its `data.js`.

Read `profile.md` before suggesting any place. The repo is public, so keep personal details out of it: no names, flights or bookings in profile.md.

## Workflow: one stage at a time, in order
1. **Frame.** What's committed: flights, nights, who's there when, rough budget, energy. Ask for whatever's missing.
2. **Scout.** No itinerary yet. For each open decision (route shape and bases first, then days, then evenings), give 2–4 real alternatives:
   - why it fits, tied to `profile.md`
   - the cost in time, money and transfers
   - what it gives up
   - one wildcard
   The traveller picks, vetoes or pushes back. Nothing goes into a zine until they have chosen it. Check facts (opening days, schedules, what Bourdain actually covered) rather than going from memory.
3. **Populate.** Build days, stops, routes, costs and photos from the chosen options only. Mark anything unconfirmed as a candidate.
4. **Book.** Fill the to-sort and booking tracker.
